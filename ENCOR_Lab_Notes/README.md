# ENCOR  — Study Notes

Cisco ENCOR-focused switching notes.

Focus: RSTP (802.1w), Rapid PVST+, MST (802.1s), STP protection, VLANs, trunks, EtherChannel and troubleshooting.

Legacy 802.1D STP is intentionally omitted from this study set.

## Contents
- `stp/` — RSTP, MST, protection and troubleshooting
- `switching/` — VLANs and trunks
- `etherchannel/` — LACP and Port-Channels
- `troubleshooting/` — practical switching checklist

## Lab method
1. Configure in CML.
2. Verify with `show` commands.
3. Break something deliberately.
4. Troubleshoot it.
5. Automate the final state with Ansible.
